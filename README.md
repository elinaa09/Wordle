#Colour	Meaning
🟩 Green	The letter is in the word and in the right spot
🟨 Yellow	The letter is in the word but in the wrong spot
🟥 Red	    The letter is not in the word

"Not in word list" means your guess isn't a valid word in the game's dictionary. Delete a few letters and try another word.


#Features
-Front page with a logo and a custom image start button
-Works with both your physical keyboard and the on-screen keyboard
-Custom image buttons for Enter and Backspace
-Large word list for answers, plus an extra list of valid guesses
-Messages for invalid words and for winning or losing
-Random word every game
